You arrive in a small frontier town with nothing but your clothes. The road outside leads into the wilds.

How this little RPG works

Explore to encounter monsters. Your Attack always deals that much damage.

Enemy damage is reduced by Armor, to a minimum of 1. Defeating enemies earns Gold and XP.

Every level gives +5 maximum HP and +1 Attack and fully heals you.

The sword and armor are permanent one-time purchases. Your progress saves automatically on this device.
